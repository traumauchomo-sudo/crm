# CRM Core Features

## 1. Contact Management
**Description:** Centralized profiles for people and organizations with key fields (name, role, company, tags, owner, and notes). Supports create, update, merge, and archive to keep the database clean and current.

**Key Capabilities**
- Custom fields and validation for industry-specific data (e.g., ARR, renewal date).
- Deduplication rules and merge previews to prevent data loss.
- Ownership, visibility, and team sharing controls.
- Bulk import/export with field mapping and error reporting.

**Primary Success Metrics**
- Duplicate rate reduction.
- Contact profile completeness score.

## 2. Lead Pipeline
**Description:** Visual stages for prospects (New, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Won/Lost) with drag-and-drop movement, stage history, and probability-based forecasting.

**Key Capabilities**
- Configurable stages, win/loss reasons, and required fields per stage.
- Pipeline value, expected close date, and weighted forecasting.
- Stage change automations (e.g., auto-create tasks on Proposal).
- Deal-level collaborators and activity rollups.

**Primary Success Metrics**
- Lead-to-opportunity conversion rate.
- Average sales cycle length.

## 3. Activity & Task Tracking
**Description:** Track calls, meetings, and follow-ups with due dates, reminders, and assignments. Provides a timeline view per contact and team to prevent missed actions.

**Key Capabilities**
- Task templates and recurring reminders.
- Calendar integration and availability scheduling.
- Mentions and notifications for handoffs.
- SLA-based alerts for overdue activities.

**Primary Success Metrics**
- Task completion on-time rate.
- Follow-up compliance rate.

## 4. Communication Logging
**Description:** Log emails, calls, and meeting outcomes with attachments and summaries, linked to contacts and deals for a full interaction history.

**Key Capabilities**
- Email threading with automatic association to contacts/deals.
- Call outcomes, sentiments, and key takeaways.
- File attachments with preview and access controls.
- Sync with external email providers (optional).

**Primary Success Metrics**
- Interaction logging coverage.
- Time-to-context for new owners.

## 5. Reporting & Dashboard
**Description:** Real-time dashboards for pipeline value, conversion rates, and team performance, with filters by owner, date range, and stage.

**Key Capabilities**
- Saved views and shareable dashboards.
- Cohort analysis by acquisition source and segment.
- Drill-down reports with export to CSV.
- Role-based visibility for sensitive metrics.

**Primary Success Metrics**
- Weekly active dashboard users.
- Forecast accuracy variance.
